# FAQs & Eligibility

## Specific Visa Pathways

### EB-1A (Extraordinary Ability)
- Global Talent Recognition
- Fast Green Card Path
- No Employer Sponsorship

### EB-2 (NIW – National Interest Waiver)
- Work Without Sponsorship
- Based on National Benefit
- Advanced Degree Advantage

### EB-3 (Skilled, Professional, and Unskilled Workers)
- Permanent U.S. Employment
- Skilled or Entry-Level Jobs
- Green Card Opportunity

### EB-4 (Special Immigrants)
- Religious Worker Visa
- Broad Eligibility Options
- Path to U.S. Residency

### EB-5 (Investor Visa Program)
- Invest and Get Residency
- Job Creation Requirement
- Family Green Card Benefit

### L1A (Executive & Manager Transfer Visa)
- Transfer to U.S. Company Branch
- Live & Work as Executive/Manager
- Direct Pathway to Green Card (EB-1C)

## Common FAQs (General extracted)
**Q: Do I need an employer sponsor for EB-1A or EB-2 NIW?**
A: No, both EB-1A and EB-2 NIW allow you to self-petition without employer sponsorship.

**Q: Can my family come with me?**
A: Yes, most employment-based green card programs like EB-1A, EB-2, EB-3, and EB-5 include dependent family members (spouse and unmarried children under 21).

**Q: How do I know which visa is right for me?**
A: We recommend booking our 30-Min Strategy Session or using our "Check Your Eligibility" tool on the website to determine the best path based on your background.
